# Images Folder

Place your image files here (PNG or JPEG format).

For your company logo:
- Recommended filename: `logo.png` or `logo.jpg` / `logo.jpeg`
- Supported formats: PNG, JPEG, JPG, WEBP, SVG
- You can upload files directly into this `/images/` folder or into `/public/images/`.
- Once uploaded, let the assistant know the filename (e.g. `logo.png`) or it will automatically detect `logo.png`, `logo.jpg`, or `logo.jpeg`.
